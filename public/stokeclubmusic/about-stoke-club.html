About Stoke Club

Newport Beach surf rock

By Stoke Club · September 28, 2026

Stoke Club is an indie surf rock band from Newport Beach, California, best known for the song Polaroid and for writing in the dreamy Mixolydian mode.

Who we are

We are a group of friends who lived together in college and shared a love for music. We started the band in 2025 and have been writing and producing our own records since. We make music to create an atmosphere you can be part of. More on our story is on the Stoke Club about page.

Our music

  • Polaroid, released June 1, 2026. 125 BPM, in A. Our best-known song.
  • Crocodile Tears, released July 3, 2026. 93 BPM on the record, in G.
  • Your Friends, coming November 6, 2026.

Our sound

Surf rock with a lot of open, major-key color, much of it written in the Mixolydian mode. If you want to know what that means, read The Mixolydian mode.
