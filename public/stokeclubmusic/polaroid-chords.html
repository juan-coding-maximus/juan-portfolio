Playing tips

  • The turnaround moves fast. E, D, C#, B come one after another at the end of each pass.
  • Every prechorus is silence. The whole band stops. Count through it and come back in together on the chorus.
  • The second chorus waits longer. After the second prechorus there is an extra pause before the chorus hits.
  • The solo reuses the verse. If you know the verse, you know the solo.
