The ranking

Focus

1Appointment setting with decision makersBetter clientsBetter seller
What it is
Booking a set time with the buyer by phone or email, then running the whole visit inside that appointment.
How it moves revenue
It buys the decision maker's attention, which a walk-in rarely gets. It is how Plameca's own reps book meetings: call ahead, then a reminder email (Sales Playbook section 10).
How I prioritize it
Grade A and B first, 91 accounts, of which only 10 ordered in 18 months.
How I improve it
One call script and one request email, approved once and reused. Mark every visit as scheduled or walk-in, so the close rate of each becomes measurable.
Evidence
None yet: nothing in the OS marks a visit as scheduled. Of 184 scheduled SDR rows, 24 are done. Measure to add: a scheduled flag on the activity.
2Restock touches on active accountsMore often
What it is
Calling or visiting the accounts that already buy, to see what needs restocking. In person I walk the shelf with them; a call saves the drive.
How it moves revenue
It is the only lever with a signal so far: the 13 active accounts with 3 or more touches placed every order the group placed.
How I prioritize it
Accounts past their own reorder gap first (the territory median is 41 days, measured 2026-08-02). Walk the shelf when a route already passes; call when it doesn't.
How I improve it
A call sheet built on reorder cadence, not on stale pipeline steps, which is what the app's "overdue" means today. Target: 3 or more touches a quarter on every active account.
Evidence
16 of 37 active accounts had no touch since 2026-07-04. Calls to active accounts: 41, one followed by an order within 14 days ($282).
3Reactivating A, B and C accounts that stoppedBetter clients
What it is
Working the high-grade accounts with no order in 18 months back to a first reorder.
How it moves revenue
159 of the 174 A, B and C accounts have not ordered in 18 months, and those grades hold 77 percent of the book's recent revenue. This is where seeing better clients pays.
How I prioritize it
Grade A first, worked in route clusters, never alphabetically. Open with "what changed" before any pitch (Sales Playbook section 3).
How I improve it
Set the number of touches before an account is parked with a dated note. I propose 3, which fills the open TODO in Sales Playbook section 6.
Evidence
Dormant accounts had no order follow any touch since logging began. Measure: first reorder per reactivated account, once fresh ERP orders land.

Improve the method

4Follow-up within two daysBetter seller
What it is
The email after a visit: what we discussed, what they asked for, the next step.
How it moves revenue
A good email sent on time keeps the visit's momentum and closes the open ask.
How I prioritize it
Every in-person visit, within two days.
How I improve it
The drafts already exist; sending is what doesn't happen. A protected 30 minutes at the start of the 4pm email block on each field day, working the Outbound queue to empty. Email templates and the drafter are the key tooling here.
Evidence
4 of 205 in-person touches followed up within 2 days. 30 drafts pending, 18 sent, 22 dismissed. No order signal yet either way.
5Week and route planningEfficiency
What it is
Choosing who I see, which day, in what order.
How it moves revenue
It multiplies everything above: more visits a day, less uncertainty between them.
How I prioritize it
Appointments anchor the week; restock and reactivation stops fill around them; prospects fill what is left.
How I improve it
One clear picture of the optimal week, not only the day. Mark each planned stop done or skipped, so plan against actual is visible.
Evidence
Median 5 visits per field day against a 10-stop plan; 15.5 miles per visit. All 51 planned stops on file still read "planned".
6Placing orders, with the upsellBetter seller
What it is
Taking a stated need and sending it to orders@nutribiotic.com, plus the client's confirmation.
How it moves revenue
Each order is a chance to add lines and reach a discount break.
How I prioritize it
Same day, every time.
How I improve it
The order draft names the next volume break (10, 15, 20 percent at 48, 96, 144 units of one SKU) or the new-SKU promotion (25, 30, 50 percent at 1, 10, 20 new SKUs), and any top seller they don't carry. Nothing computes either today.
Evidence
Average order $237 to $308 and 7.1 to 7.6 lines, flat since 2024.
7First visit: discovery and sampleBetter seller
What it is
The discovery conversation at a new door, and a sample left behind.
How it moves revenue
It is where qualification happens: a well-qualified first visit decides whether the account is worth a second.
How I prioritize it
Prospects scored 50 or higher get a visit; under 50 get a call or email.
How I improve it
Log every sample dropped, and put a follow-up call 7 days later onto a route. Run the golden minute from Sales Playbook section 10.
Evidence
1 new account since logging began: 3 touches, 15 days to first order. Sample drops are not recorded anywhere yet.
8Prospect search and scoring near A, B and C anchorsBetter clients
What it is
Finding stores and clinics not yet in the book, scoring them, and placing them on the map.
How it moves revenue
The book does not yet hold every potential client. New revenue, even small, beats zero.
How I prioritize it
Prospects near an A, B or C account first, so they land on a route I already drive.
How I improve it
Score each prospect by distance to an anchor account, and grade the 127 accounts with no grade yet.
Evidence
34 prospects landed from a Places search; 1 touched, none ordered.
9Area prospecting on route daysBetter clientsEfficiency
What it is
Already in Newport Beach, finding the high-fit prospects nearby.
How it moves revenue
Adds stops to a drive already paid for.
How I prioritize it
Only to fill a day that is short of stops.
How I improve it
Each planned day fills from 5 toward 8 stops with the nearest scored prospects.
Evidence
Median 5 visits per field day.
10New-client onboardingBetter seller
What it is
Getting a new store from yes to a first order on its way.
How it moves revenue
Today ordering works well only for existing clients. A clear path keeps a new yes from stalling.
How I prioritize it
Every new account.
How I improve it
One documented page: newaccounts@nutribiotic.com, the customer requirements, the $75 minimum, COD until credit is set then Net 30, and the new-store promotion for the first order.
Evidence
No onboarding document exists in the repo.
11Cold outbound email for awarenessBetter clients
What it is
High-volume email to the coldest leads, not waiting for replies. Two flavors: existing clients and potential clients.
How it moves revenue
Indirectly: the subject line is read, so the next call lands warmer.
How I prioritize it
After the work above, in batches.
How I improve it
Two approved templates, each send logged as a touch, and replies linked to the email that earned them.
Evidence
18 drafts sent; 3 emails came back, none linked to a send. Measure to add: reply linkage on outbound drafts.
12In-store staff trainingBetter seller
What it is
Short trainings that teach a store's staff to recommend the product.
How it moves revenue
Sell-through: the shelf empties faster and the reorder comes sooner. Armando named it, with follow-up, as the habit he repeats most.
How I prioritize it
A pilot at 2 grade A accounts.
How I improve it
Measure each pilot by a reorder within 60 days.
Evidence
Not piloted yet (In-Store Program section 4).

Test with the budget

18TikTok Shop affiliatesBetter clients
What it is
Paying creators a commission on every TikTok Shop sale of NutriBiotic, with sample sends to the ones who post.
How it moves revenue
It is the one activity where return is read straight off the dashboard: sales per creator against commission and sample cost. NutriBiotic has $10,000 for paid media, so I can set a ROAS floor before spending and stop what misses it.
How I prioritize it
Supplements are priced at 15 to 25 percent commission on the platform, and open collaboration runs lower than targeted collaboration. Two SKUs only, so every creator pushes the same two products and the read stays clean. Start with a small creator list on targeted terms, then widen only what clears the floor. The floor counts commission, not only ad spend.
How I improve it
One page per creator: sales, commission paid, samples sent, ROAS. A weekly read, and cut any creator under the floor after two weeks.
Evidence
None yet. NutriBiotic has no TikTok Shop seller account, and the OS holds no TikTok Shop sales, spend or creator data. Next step: open the seller account and get the two SKUs approved. Measure to add: spend, commission and sales per creator, in one table.
19Reacher App for creator outreachEfficiency
What it is
The software that runs TikTok Shop affiliate outreach: finds creators, sends invites and messages, queues sample requests, tracks each creator's sales.
How it moves revenue
It turns activity 18 from a manual chore into a measured channel. Its reporting is what makes ROAS per creator visible without a spreadsheet.
How I prioritize it
Start on the Starter plan, $199 a month, and move to Pro, $599 a month, only if outreach volume or AI creator search is the limit. Pro adds AI creator search, automated follow-ups and unlimited daily messages.
How I improve it
Run it for one month on one product line. Compare the month's creator sales to the plan cost plus commission.
Evidence
Plan prices from reacherapp.com/pricing, read 2026-10-05. No trial or spend has started, and Reacher works on a TikTok Shop account, so it waits on the seller account. Buying the plan is mine to approve, from the $10,000.
