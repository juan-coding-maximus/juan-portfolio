# Enterprise Chains · the hit list

The rule of this document, same as the rest of the shelf: **a line is either
sourced or marked TODO.** Nothing here is written as a fact unless it traces
to a field visit already logged in the OS or a live source cited below.

This is a separate hit list from the territory playbook, because the win
here isn't one store, it's one relationship that opens several doors. It
splits into two tracks because "get in" means something completely
different depending on who actually says yes.

**Track A, store-level**: the person behind the counter, or one buyer for
that one location, can say yes. This is field work at volume, no
application to build.

**Track B, central/HQ**: the store cannot say yes. A category buyer, an
incubator program, or a corporate vendor portal decides, usually for every
store in the chain at once. This needs a packet, not a visit, and one
open question decides whether it's even Juan's to run (see "Before Track
B" below).

Last built: 2026-08-24, from `nb_activities` and live web research.

### Whole Foods Market
**Status**: `chain_excluded` since 2026-08-04, no visit logged anywhere,
including Silver Lake.
**Tip**: don't cold-walk a store. The **Local and Regional Producer
Program** is the real entry point, it's built to get a first meeting with
a buyer and gives explicit preference to brands local to the region. The
**LEAP program** (launched 2022) is a second door: a six-month cohort for
small/emerging suppliers with mentorship and a shelf placement at the end,
in the brand's home region. NutriBiotic already has the track record LEAP
is designed for, this is worth an actual application, not a store visit.
**Links**: [Information for Potential Suppliers](https://www.wholefoodsmarket.com/company-info/information-potential-suppliers) · [Local Producer Loan Program](https://www.wholefoodsmarket.com/mission-values/local-producer-loan-program)
**Process**: Potential Supplier Form on the site, minimum $2M product
liability insurance naming Whole Foods as additional insured, products
must clear their Quality Standards.
**Success story (2022-2023)**: the first LEAP cohort graduated 9 local
producers, 3 launched into select stores, including CHKP Foods, a
plant-based yogurt brand.
**Sources**: [Getting Into Whole Foods overview](https://femfounded.org/reference/getting-into-whole-foods/), [Whole Foods vendor requirements](https://blog.getopener.ai/whole-foods-vendor-requirements-shelf-space)

### Sephora
**Status**: Beverly Hills visited 8/19, staff confirmed no store-level

American-incorporated company required.
**Success story (2023)**: Brown Girl Jane (Malaika Jones), first Black
woman-founded fine fragrance brand to enter Sephora, launched March 2023
out of the 2023 Accelerate cohort.
**Sources**: [2023 Accelerate cohort announcement](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20221206005483/en/Sephora-Announces-Participants-for-2023-Accelerate-Brand-Incubator-Program-With-Continued-Focus-on-BIPOC-Brand-Founders-and-New-Programming)
