# Onboarding Kit · new rep, new territory

Goal: a new hire runs their territory solo within **2 weeks** of start. The kit
exists so onboarding is a runbook, not an apprenticeship; Juan's time goes into
ride-alongs and judgment, not into explaining where files live.

## What a new hire receives on day 1

1. **Their territory bundle**: the dossier export run for their owner id, on their laptop. One folder, phone-readable account pages, the master table, route plans. ([`onboarding/stack.md`](onboarding/stack.md), step 1.)
2. **Sales OS access** scoped to their book.
3. **The three playbooks**: sales, seasonal marketing, in-store program.
4. **GOALS.md**, so they see the system they are joining and where it is headed.

## The two weeks

| Days | What happens | Exit test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1-2 | Read the playbooks; learn the OS tabs; learn the dossier layout | Can find any account's last order, top products, and named contact in under a minute |
| 3-5 | Ride-along days on Juan's routes; new hire logs the visit notes | Notes are usable: who, what was asked, what happened, next step |
| 6-8 | Reverse ride-along: new hire leads the doors, Juan observes | Runs the visit loop (dossier, named contact, lifecycle-specific ask, logged note) unprompted |
| 9-10 | New hire plans their own first week from the overdue list and route clusters | The plan respects clusters, call-first flags, and cadence |
| 11-14 | Solo days; daily 15-min debrief | Two consecutive solo days needing no correction on process |

## Rules that transfer with the kit

- No fabrication: unknown is a finding; nothing invented at a door or in a note.
- The CRM is shared: writes are scoped to your own book, always.
- Customer data stays in the employer's blast radius: never in git, never in chat tools.
- Sends are human-gated: drafts are cheap, sending is a decision.

## Iteration

Every onboarding produces edits to this kit and to the playbooks (v2 rule: the
playbook must survive a second person). The kit is judged by one number: days to
solo, target under 14.
