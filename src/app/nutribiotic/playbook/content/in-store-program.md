# In-Store Program · v0

Four tactics, each with a cost line and a measure before it runs. The program's
rule mirrors the marketing playbook: **no tactic scales until its pilot showed its
number.** Store selection always starts from the OS: potential grade, lifecycle,
and whether the door is on a route we already drive.

Targets below are `proposed` until confirmed in GOALS.md.

## 1. Endcap and seasonal shelf promos

- **What**: an endcap or secondary placement of the seasonal anchor line (immune in fall, mental clarity at finals, electrolytes in summer), with shelf-talkers and a simple special.
- **Pilot**: 5 stores by 2026-11-30, chosen for grade B+ potential and an existing relationship from the visit loop.
- **Cost line**: display materials, promo discount, any placement fee the store asks.
- **Measure**: the store's featured-line order lift during the placement window vs its own baseline (`nb_order_lines`), plus a photo log of every placement.

## 2. Monthly mailed brochure buys

- **What**: a paid spot in the physically mailed monthly discount sales brochures stores distribute.
- **Pilot**: one placement, in a month where the wheel's theme lines up.
- **Cost line**: the placement price, on the receipt, reconciled like any other spend.
- **Measure**: orders of the featured products from accounts in the brochure's distribution area during the window; honest caveat in the readout that attribution here is directional, not clean.

## 3. Manager merch and goodwill kits

- **What**: branded tumblers, branded pins, free product for store managers and key floor staff. Goodwill is a real asset in a shelf-space negotiation; it is also unmeasurable order-by-order, so it is budgeted as relationship spend, not performance spend.
- **Target**: kits in the hands of 20 store managers by 2026-12-31, logged per store (who got what, when) so the second visit builds on the first.
- **Cost line**: unit cost times kits; free product logged at cost.
- **Measure**: coverage (kits delivered vs planned) and the relationship note on the account, not an order-lift claim we cannot honestly make.

## 4. Staff knowledge one-pagers

- **What**: a one-page, phone-photographable sheet per anchor line: what it is, who it is for, the two questions customers actually ask, the answer a floor employee can give. Left with staff on visits, refreshed each season.
- **Rule**: product claims on these sheets come from the label and official product material only. No invented health claims, ever; this is the no-fabrication principle with regulatory teeth.
- **Measure**: coverage per route cluster, and whether staff at revisited stores remembered the line (logged in the visit note).

## Budget posture

Every tactic's spend lands on a receipt and reconciles into the department's
books. The Q2 QBR decides scale-up per tactic on its measure, not on enthusiasm.
