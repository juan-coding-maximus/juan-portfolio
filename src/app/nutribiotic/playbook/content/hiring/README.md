## Role scorecard (draft, to be confirmed)

- **Mission**: own a defined territory book and grow its trailing-12-month ordering-account count, running the house playbook.
- **Outcomes (first 6 months)**: `proposed` solo within 14 days; every overdue account in their book touched within 60 days; reactivations logged monthly.
- **Competencies**: door comfort, note discipline (the system runs on honest notes), route sense, no-fabrication instinct: says "I don't know" at a door rather than inventing.
- **The real interview**: a paid ride-along day. Watch the doors, not the resume.

## Agentic help (planned, human-gated)

- Interview scheduling proposals through the calendar gate.

Nothing outward (posting the job, contacting a candidate) goes without Juan's
explicit approval per item.
